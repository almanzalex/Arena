## Generalization evidence

- Trainer registry: behavior cloning and return-weighted regression are separate
  registered cases. The return-weighted fixture learns the high-return action from a
  deliberately conflicting dataset and repeats to the same policy digest.
- Dataset/resume: two materializations produce identical split metadata and identity.
  A 20-epoch checkpoint resumed to 40 epochs produces the exact policy digest and loss
  history of an uninterrupted 40-epoch run. Payload mutation is refused without output.
- Lifecycle: the role-resolved fixture removes `agent_0`, then admits that same ID again
  on the boundary where `agent_1` leaves and `agent_2` joins. Segment history retains
  both `agent_0` lifetimes.
- OpenEnv: the vector-coordination fixture crosses the actual FastAPI/WebSocket OpenEnv
  service with a typed Box contract and matches its native task trace.
- OpenSpiel: Connect Four, Kuhn Poker, and Matrix RPS exercise deterministic sequential
  perfect-information, chance/imperfect-information, and simultaneous semantics.
- Gimitest: provider lineage survives an explicit subprocess request/response boundary
  using a declared Python interpreter and timeout.
- Stores/authenticity: HF, OCI, W&B, and MLflow simulations each verify push and pull,
  restore the same policy identity, and preserve a detached Ed25519 signature.
- Generic store verification: policy, ordinary directory, and single-object artifacts
  recompute restored identity rather than trusting the descriptor.
- Evaluation: worker threads execute independent cells concurrently while result order,
  cell IDs, assignments, and seed mapping stay deterministic.
